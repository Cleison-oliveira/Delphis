## 📦 Core Libraries & Backend Engines

### Texture Extraction & Archival
- **[DolphinTextureExtraction-tool](https://github.com/Venomalia/DolphinTextureExtraction-tool)** by Venomalia — GameCube & Wii texture dumping and tonemap combining.
- **[AuroraLib](https://github.com/Venomalia/AuroraLib.Compression)** by Venomalia — Disc image and archive decompression library.
- **[AFSLib](https://github.com/MaikelChan/AFSLib)** by MaikelChan — CRI AFS archive reader (MIT License).
- **[Hack.io](https://github.com/SuperHackio/Hack.io)** by SuperHackio — RARC, U8, BTI, TPL, TEX1, BMD, and BDL format support.
- **[cpk-tools](https://github.com/ConnorKrammer/cpk-tools)** by ConnorKrammer — CRIWARE CPK archive handling.
- **[ImageSharp](https://github.com/SixLabors/ImageSharp)** by SixLabors — Graphics API.
- **[HashDepot](https://github.com/ssg/HashDepot)** by ssg — xxHash Dolphin texture hashing.
- **[SevenZipExtractor](https://github.com/adoconnection/SevenZipExtractor)** by adoconnection — 7Zip archive reader.
- **[Puyo Tools](https://github.com/nickworonekin/puyotools)** by nickworonekin — ONE GVMH, GBIX, GCIX, GVRT formats.
- **[Switch-Toolbox](https://github.com/KillzXGaming/Switch-Toolbox)** by KillzXGaming — PTLG format reference.
- **[Rune-Factory-Frontier-Tools](https://github.com/master801/Rune-Factory-Frontier-Tools)** by master801 — NLCM archive reference.
- **[MODConv](https://github.com/intns/MODConv)** by intns — MOD format reference.
- **[mpbintools](https://github.com/gamemasterplc/mpbintools)** & **[mpatbtools](https://github.com/gamemasterplc/mpatbtools)** by gamemasterplc — BIN_MP and ATB format references.
- **[BrawlCrate](https://github.com/soopercool101/BrawlCrate)** by soopercool101 — ARC0 format reference.
- **[Pitfall / Helco](https://github.com/Helco/Pitfall)** by Helco — LFXT format reference.
- **[Endless-Ocean-Files-Converter](https://github.com/NiV-L-A/Endless-Ocean-Files-Converter)** by NiV-L-A — TDL0 format reference.

### AI Upscaling & Compression
- **[Real-ESRGAN Vulkan NCNN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN)** by Xintao Wang et al. — High-performance neural network image upscaling.
- **[NVIDIA Texture Tools (NVTT)](https://github.com/castano/nvidia-texture-tools)** by Ignacio Castano — High-performance DirectDraw Surface (DDS) hardware block compressor.
- **[ImageMagick](https://imagemagick.org/)** — Versatile digital image manipulation suite.
- **[Pillow](https://python-pillow.org/)** by Alex Clark and Contributors — Python Imaging Library.
- **[PyQt6](https://riverbankcomputing.com/software/pyqt/)** by Riverbank Computing — Python Qt bindings.

### Desktop & UI
- **[KDE Community](https://kde.org/)** — Breeze Icon Theme & visual style standards.
